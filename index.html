A City Through Time

Four Seasons, One Beautiful City

Kabul's continental climate brings noticeable seasonal changes, transforming its scenery throughout the year.

MARCH – MAY

Spring

Blossoming flowers, greener landscapes, and fresh mountain air bring renewed color to the city.

JUNE – AUGUST

Summer

Long sunny days and warm weather make summer a distinctive part of Kabul's annual cycle.

SEPTEMBER – NOVEMBER

Autumn

Cooler days and golden tones give the surrounding landscapes a calm, warm atmosphere.

DECEMBER – FEBRUARY

Winter

Cold temperatures and occasional snowfall can cover nearby mountains in beautiful white scenery.

People & Traditions

The Heart of Kabul Is Its People

Kabul is home to communities with diverse ethnic, linguistic, and cultural backgrounds. Its identity has been shaped by generations of shared experiences, traditions, and connections.

Pashtuns, Tajiks, Hazaras, Uzbeks, and other communities have longstanding connections to Kabul and the wider region. Dari and Pashto are among the main languages heard in the city.

Islam is the predominant religion in Kabul, with Sunni and Shia Muslim communities. The city's cultural life also reflects different regional traditions, family customs, food, poetry, and celebrations.
